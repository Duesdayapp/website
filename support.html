Questions

When does Duesday launch?

January 2027, before winter term starts. Join the waitlist on the home page and we'll email you the day it's out.

Which devices does it work on?

Mac, iPad, and iPhone. One purchase covers all three, and your plan and notes sync between them. Apple Pencil works on iPad.

How much will it cost?

The first 7 days are free. After that you pay only until your last exam, with no auto-renew, and it gets cheaper per month the longer you go. Exact prices go up at launch.

Can high school students use it?

Not yet. Duesday is for university and college students for now.

Does Duesday guarantee a grade?

No. It shows an estimate of where you're headed as a range, and what would move it up. You still have to do the studying. Duesday just makes it obvious what to do next.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete my account. It removes your account and everything in it, for good.

How do I get off the waitlist?

Email hello@duesdayapp.com from the address you signed up with and we'll remove you.
