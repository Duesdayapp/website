Who's building it

Hi, I'm Andre.

I'm a business student at Brandon University, in Manitoba. Every term starts the same way for me: a stack of syllabi, deadlines everywhere, and a plan that only exists in my head.

I couldn't find one app that handled all of it, so I'm building it. Duesday is my first app. I had no coding experience when I started, and I'm building it in public: real progress, real numbers, real mistakes.

If you join the waitlist, you'll hear from me once, on launch day. Questions before then? hello@duesdayapp.com comes straight to me.

Fair questions

Before you join.

Which devices does it work on?

Mac, iPad, and iPhone. One subscription covers all three. There's no Android or web version for now.

Who is it for?

Students 14 and up in Canada and the USA: high school, college, university, and anyone studying for an exam like the CPA, LSAT, or MCAT. Any subject.

What does the AI read? Is my stuff private?

Duesday's AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) only reads what a job needs: your syllabus, to find your deadlines, and the text of your notes and slides if you ask for a quiz. It asks you first. Your study plan is made by Duesday's own code, not by AI. Your calendar's busy times are read on your device and never leave it. No ads, no selling your data, no chat with strangers.

How much does it cost?

$6.99 a month or $39.99 a year, in Canadian dollars. There's no free trial at launch, and nothing is charged until you subscribe in the app. Joining the waitlist is free.

When does it launch?

January 2027, in time for winter term. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the day it's out.

Will it promise me a grade?

No. Nobody can honestly promise you a grade. Duesday shows where you stand and about what you need on what's left. The studying is still yours.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. You can type everything. On iPad you can also write by hand with Apple Pencil, and on iPhone with your finger. The Mac shows your handwriting but can't write it.

What if my syllabus is a mess?

Give it a PDF, a photo, or a Word doc. You check everything Duesday found before a plan is made, and you can fix anything it got wrong. No date yet? Mark it TBD and fill it in later.
