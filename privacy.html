Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Draft. This covers the waitlist today and what the app will do at launch. The final version goes up before Duesday is in the App Store.

Duesday is a study and note-taking app for university and college students, made by Andre Balcaen in Brandon, Manitoba, Canada. Here's what we collect, why, and how to get rid of it. Questions? Email hello@duesdayapp.com.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we keep:

  • your email address
  • the school you typed in
  • your invite code, and who invited you (if anyone)
  • when you joined

We use this to email you when Duesday launches, to work out your spot on the list, and to see which schools to reach first. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

This website doesn't use cookies, ads, or trackers. After you join, your email is saved in your own browser so the page can show your spot when you come back. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may log basic technical details like IP addresses to keep its service secure.

The app (at launch)

To make your study plan, Duesday stores:

  • Your account: email, school, and the settings you choose.
  • Your courses: the syllabi, slides, and class notes you upload, and what Duesday reads from them (tests, dates, weights, topics).
  • Your studying: your study plan, sessions, minutes, check-ins, marks you enter, and your notes, including handwriting.
  • Your schedule: class times and busy times. If you allow calendar access, Duesday reads when you're busy on your device to avoid those times.

AI

Duesday uses Anthropic's Claude to read your syllabus and materials, build your plan, and write explanations. Before anything is sent to the AI for the first time, the app asks you and explains what's shared. For handwritten notes, only the recognized text is sent, never the drawing. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.

Grades and leaderboards

  • Grade estimates use your own studying and marks, plus anonymous, combined results from past Duesday students in the same course. Nobody ever sees another student's marks.
  • Leaderboards show your first name, last initial, and school. You can hide yourself from leaderboards in Settings.

Who helps us run Duesday

  • Supabase: stores accounts and data.
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes and launch emails.
  • Anthropic: AI features (see above).
  • Apple: payments, notifications, and Sign in with Apple.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

These services may store data in Canada or the United States.

Your choices

  • Leave the waitlist: email hello@duesdayapp.com and we'll remove you.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings, then Delete my account. It removes your account and everything in it.
  • See or fix your info: email us and we'll help.
  • Turn off AI note-reading any time in Settings.

Students only

Duesday is for university and college students. It isn't meant for high school students. If you think one has joined, email us and we'll remove them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a big way, we'll say so in the app or by email before it takes effect.
